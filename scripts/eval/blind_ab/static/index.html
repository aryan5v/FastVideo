How to vote

Each pair shows the same AI-generated clip rendered by two different video decoders. Composition and motion are identical; only the image quality differs (sharpness, texture, fine detail, color, flicker). Pick the one that looks better.

Use the Audio control (or M) to choose which side you hear.

Tip: press Z for a 1:1 zoom on fine detail.

A session is 12 comparisons. You can stop any time — every vote counts.

Your name is saved with each vote.

Thank you!
